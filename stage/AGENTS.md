# Stage Plot and Input List

## General

1. The page must support phone, desktop, portrait, landscape, and print layouts.
1. All content must remain legible and contained without clipping or unintended overlap.
1. Spacing and padding should be comfortable but compact.
1. Intentional stage-element relationships must remain consistent across screen and print layouts.
1. The complete document must print on one landscape page.

## Stage Geometry

1. Ireland must occupy the full front row, with the other performers and equipment behind her.
1. Use the horizontal gap between adjacent performer boxes as the standard gap between non-overlapping stage elements.
1. Mike's row, the Holly/Eric/Andrew row, and Ireland's row must be separated by approximately the standard gap.
1. The Keys box must intentionally overlap the lower edges of Holly's and Eric's boxes.
1. Mike's box must match the height of the adjacent POWER and NEAT Split/XR18 stack.
1. Holly, Eric, and Andrew must have clear, balanced vertical padding without making their boxes unnecessarily tall.
1. Ireland's box must communicate her full-width position without using excessive height.
1. Ireland's label must be centered above her microphone stand with visible clearance.
1. Ireland's microphone stand must remain centered against the bottom of her box.
1. Ireland's box must end approximately one standard gap above the stage-orientation line.
1. The Stage Plot heading must use the same top inset as the other panel headings without repositioning the stage elements.

## Landscape

1. The Stage Plot and Input & Patch List panels must have equal outer heights.
1. Their shared height must be determined by the patch-list content; the Stage Plot must not make the row taller.
1. Available stage height should be distributed through performer-box padding while preserving required gaps and overlaps.
1. The panels must begin directly below the header, with surplus page height left below the content.
1. The Stage Plot panel and all its contents must remain inside its grid column and must never overlap the Input & Patch List.

## Portrait

1. The gap below the Mike/equipment row must match the standard gap.

## Production Notes

1. Production Notes must always read in this order: House Provides, Band Provides, then Timing.
1. The three sections must wrap naturally without changing their reading order.
1. At three columns, all three sections must appear on one row in reading order.
1. At two columns, House Provides and Band Provides must occupy the first row, with Timing beginning the second row at the left beneath House Provides.
1. At one column, House Provides, Band Provides, and Timing must stack vertically in reading order.

## Print

1. The stage/patch row and Production Notes must use their content height without a large empty band between them.
1. The Stage Plot must remain contained within its column and must not overlap the Input & Patch List.
1. The NEAT Split/XR18 box must contain all four lines with visible internal padding and must not collide with POWER.
1. "DESTINATION TRANSMITTER" must remain on one line without clipping.
1. Any unavoidable unused page height must remain at the bottom.
